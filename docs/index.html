Core Features

Everything a strategist
needs, built in

From live news ingestion to AI-powered deep dives — the full research workflow in one professional interface.

📡
Live News Feed
Real-time headlines from NewsAPI, auto-categorized across 9 strategy lenses. Category tab bar with article counts — filter the feed in one click.
🧠
AI Intelligence Profiles
Tag articles to companies and generate a 9-section investment-grade brief — including Signal Scores (1–10) with radar chart and AI-written rationale for each rating.
⚡
Battle View
Select two saved articles for a head-to-head comparison — interactive RadarChart, per-metric progress bars, and automatic scoring on 6 dimensions.
📋
Strategy Matrix
Clip and annotate articles with research notes. Company-tag any article to group it into AI profiles. All data persists in localStorage — no account needed.
🔗
Paste Any URL
Drop any article link into the feed. Preview metadata is fetched automatically. Pasted articles support every feature — save, tag, profile, battle.
⚖️
Profile Comparison
Side-by-side two AI profiles with an AI synthesis highlighting the sharpest strategic contrasts, shared risks, and relative competitive positioning.
8 Research Tabs

A workspace for every
part of your research

Every tab is a distinct tool. Switch between them with one click — all state persists across sessions.

01
Research
Live feed + saved matrix side by side. The core workspace.
02
Morning Briefing
One click → AI-generated daily digest from the live feed. Cached so it's instant on revisit.
03
Watchlist
Track companies and keywords. Live match counts update automatically as the feed refreshes.
04
Trends
Category bar charts, company coverage, save-activity timeline, heat grid, and sentiment chart per company.
05
Timeline
All saved articles in chronological order, grouped by week. Filter by category and company.
06
Comparison Lab
Compare up to 4 company profiles — overlay radar, score breakdown table, top/bottom signal dimensions.
07
Reports
Build polished research reports. AI executive summary + copy as Markdown or print to PDF.
08
Thesis Builder
Group articles into investment theses. AI synthesises evidence for, against, and what must be true.
How it works

Research workflow,
end to end

The dashboard guides you from raw news to polished deliverables in five clear steps.

01
Collect articles
Browse the live feed filtered by category, or paste any URL. Add articles to your saved matrix with one click.
02
Tag and annotate
Tag each article to a company. Add your own research notes — they feed directly into the AI profile prompt.
03
Generate AI profiles
Hit "Generate Profile" — the AI writes a 9-section brief with Signal Scores, a radar chart, and per-score rationale.
04
Analyse across tools
Switch to Trends for charts, Timeline to track how a story evolved, or Comparison Lab to overlay up to 4 companies.
05
Build a thesis or report
Group articles into structured theses in Thesis Builder, or export a polished research report as Markdown or PDF.
AI Engine

radar charts, locally

Quick start
# clone & install
git clone https://github.com/jmitchell26-netizen/Strategy-Dashboard-.git
cd strategy-dashboard && npm install

# set your keys (free Groq key works great)
cp .env.example .env  # edit VITE_OLLAMA_* with Groq credentials

# or run fully local (free)
ollama pull llama3.1:8b
npm run dev
