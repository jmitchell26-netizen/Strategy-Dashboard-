Features

Everything a strategist
needs, built in

From live news to AI-powered deep dives, the dashboard covers the full research workflow in one place.

📡
Live News Feed
Pulls real-time headlines from NewsAPI across categories — Tech, Finance, AI, Geopolitics, and more. Auto-categorized and searchable.
🧠
AI Company Profiles
Tag saved articles to companies, hit Generate, and get a structured intelligence brief — executive overview, risks, opportunities, competitive signals.
⚡
Battle View
Select two articles and compare them head-to-head on an interactive RadarChart — credibility, market impact, recency, novelty, and more metrics.
📋
Strategy Matrix
Clip and annotate articles with your own research notes. All data persists in localStorage — no account required, no cloud lock-in.
🔗
Paste Your Own Links
Drop any article URL into the feed. Microlink fetches the preview automatically. Use all the same features — save, tag, AI profile, Battle View.
⚖️
Profile Comparison
Side-by-side two AI-generated company profiles with an AI synthesis layer that highlights sharpest contrasts, risks, and strategic divergence.
How it works

Research workflow,
end to end

The dashboard guides you from raw news to polished strategic intelligence in four simple steps.

01
Collect articles
Browse the live feed or paste your own URLs. Tag articles to companies with a single click.
02
Annotate with notes
Add your own research notes to any saved article in the Strategy Matrix. These notes feed directly into the AI.
03
Generate AI profiles
Hit "Generate Profile" — the AI synthesizes all clippings and notes into a structured intelligence brief.
04
Compare & decide
Battle articles or compare full company profiles side-by-side with AI synthesis to sharpen your conclusions.

cd strategy-dashboard && npm install

# set your keys
cp .env.example .env  # then edit .env

# run (free local AI)
ollama pull llama3.1:8b
npm run dev
